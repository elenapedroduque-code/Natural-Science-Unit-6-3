Types of Rocks

3rd Grade Social Science – Let’s learn about rocks in English!

What is a rock?

Rock is a solid substance that contains one or more minerals.

There are three types of rock that form in different ways:

  • Igneous rock
  • Sedimentary rock
  • Metamorphic rock

Imagine these pictures in your mind:

  • Igneous rock: hot lava becoming hard rock.
  • Sedimentary rock: many layers of sand and stones.
  • Metamorphic rock: folded rock changed by heat and pressure.

The three types of rock

Igneous rock Hot rock

Hot, liquid rock (magma and lava) cools down and becomes solid rock.

Examples: granite, basalt, pumice

(Think of a lava flow that becomes hard rock.)

Sedimentary rock Layers

Sand, stones and other particles form layers that slowly become solid rock.

Examples: sandstone, limestone, shale

(Think of many sedimentary rock layers, one on top of another.)

Metamorphic rock Change

Rock changes under a lot of heat and pressure. It forms new types of rock.

Examples: marble, slate, quartzite

(Think of folded marble that has changed shape.)
